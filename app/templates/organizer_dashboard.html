Statistical Normalization Methodology

How Normalization Works:

Evaluator scores are standardized per judge (calculating Z-scores based on their scoring distribution) and mapped back to the 1–5 scale. Projects are ranked by calibrated score, eliminating bias from harsh or lenient reviewers. Zero-variance judges contribute neutrally.

Platform Role Governance & Access Reference

JudgeForge enforces strict role separation across public, participant, evaluation, and operational tiers.

5 System Roles
Participant

Submit projects, manage team members, edit drafts prior to deadline, view submission confirmation and published results.

Judge

Evaluate assigned track submissions on 1–5 rubric cards, submit private feedback, save reviews independently.

Organizer

Configure competition parameters, assign judges to tracks, monitor deliberation quotas, review normalization standings, publish results.

Administrator

Platform-level governance, RBAC user role administration with self-lockout defense, multi-event oversight, and system status monitoring.

Visitor

Unauthenticated public access. Browse showcase gallery, inspect project profiles and media, view published podium winners.
