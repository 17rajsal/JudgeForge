Statistical Normalization Methodology

How Normalization Works:

Evaluator scores are standardized per judge (calculating Z-scores based on their scoring distribution) and mapped back to the 1–5 scale. Projects are ranked by calibrated score, eliminating bias from harsh or lenient reviewers. Zero-variance judges contribute neutrally.
