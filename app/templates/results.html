How Winners Are Determined — Fair Scoring & Normalization Methodology

JudgeForge utilizes a deterministic four-phase pipeline to ensure all submissions are evaluated fairly across diverse judging panels:

1. SCORE — Calibrated Rubric (1–5)
Judges evaluate projects on a 1–5 rubric across core dimensions (Functionality, Innovation, Code Quality, Live Demo, Pitch Deck).
2. WEIGHT — Criterion Weights
Criteria weights are applied to produce a weighted raw score prior to normalization.
3. CALIBRATE — Z-Score Standardization & Zero-Variance Proofing
Judge scoring distributions are standardized via Z-score calibration to neutralize naturally harsh or lenient tendencies. Zero-variance judges contribute neutrally.
4. RANK — Rescaled to 1–5 Scale
Projects are ranked by calibrated score. Normalized values are mapped back to the 1–5 scale to determine podium winners.
Peer scores remain strictly isolated until official publication to guarantee independent and tamper-free evaluation.
